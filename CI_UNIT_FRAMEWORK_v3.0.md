\#\# STEP 2: HOW WILL YOU MEASURE STUDENT SUCCESS?

\#\#\# 1\. Diagnostic Assessments  
\* \*\*Requirements:\*\* Exactly 1 diagnostic assessment designed to determine where to begin instruction and identify prior knowledge and student misconceptions\[cite: 12\].  
\* \*\*Design Focus:\*\* Must assess the core skills in the selected standards and unit theme\[cite: 12\].

\#\#\# 2\. Summative Assessments  
\* \*\*Requirements:\*\* 1 comprehensive summative assessment plus criteria for success (e.g., rubric, criteria chart)\[cite: 12\].  
\* \*\*Design Focus:\*\* Open-ended, complex performance tasks that evaluate deep understanding of big ideas and measure all selected standards. All unit work should scaffold directly toward this culminating task\[cite: 12\].
