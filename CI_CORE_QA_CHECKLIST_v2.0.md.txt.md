\# CRITICAL INQUIRY AI QUALITY ASSURANCE CHECKLIST

\#\# Pre-Release Validation for Units and Lessons (v2.0)

Use this checklist after generation and before classroom deployment. A plan must not be released when a critical requirement fails.

\---

\#\# Status Vocabulary

Use one status value for every check:

\- \`PASS\`: Requirement is satisfied.  
\- \`WARNING\`: Requirement needs teacher judgment, clarification, or minor revision.  
\- \`FAIL\`: Critical requirement is not satisfied; release is blocked.  
\- \`NOT\_APPLICABLE\`: Requirement does not apply, with a written justification.

Do not leave a status blank.

\#\# Release Rule

A plan must not be released until all critical failures are resolved.

\#\#\# Critical source and verification failures

\- Invented quotations, statistics, dates, captions, page numbers, or archival details.  
\- Unverified or contested quotations appearing in student-facing materials.  
\- Missing Source Verification Table for an anchor or secondary source.  
\- Misidentified, fabricated, or unverifiable source metadata.  
\- Uncertainty or contested interpretation presented as settled fact.

\#\#\# Critical alignment and inquiry failures

\- Phase minutes do not sum exactly to the declared period.  
\- Missing source tension when critical inquiry is claimed as the lesson purpose.  
\- No provisional claim where claim formation is a stated target.  
\- No visible claim revision where claim revision is a stated target.  
\- Predetermined conclusion that prevents responsible disagreement.  
\- Assessment does not measure the stated objective or standard.  
\- Ethical limit is absent where the task makes an interpretive, systemic, or action claim.

\#\#\# Critical safety and access failures

\- Sensitive content is triggered but required safeguards are missing.  
\- Students are required to disclose personal trauma, identity, or community experience.  
\- The task is inaccessible and provides no pathway preserving the intellectual target.  
\- Public sharing creates foreseeable risk without consent, privacy protection, or an alternative response mode.

\#\#\# Critical timing failures

\- Any phase exceeds 35 minutes without an explicit break, transition, or change in social mode.  
\- A 20-minute instructional window contains no formative check for understanding.  
\- The lesson cannot reasonably be completed in the declared period.

\#\#\# Critical tactic-compliance failures

\- A selected tactic is not present in the authorized catalog.  
\- A selected tactic is missing required schema fields.  
\- A tactic is used outside its phase, text type, timing, or grouping constraints without justification.  
\- Required instructional moves are omitted.  
\- The tactic’s output product does not align with the lesson artifact.  
\- A listed contraindication is ignored without explicit justification.
