\#\#\# 10\. Formative Assessment Matrix (10-Point Rubric)

| Criterion | 2 \- Proficient | 1 \- Approaching | 0 \- Not Yet |  
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |  
| \*\*Initiation / Claim Quality\*\* | Clear, debatable claim directly answering CIQ. | Claim present but vague or broad. | Missing claim or non-responsive. |  
| \*\*Use of Evidence\*\* | Cites specific, relevant evidence with page/line context. | Evidence referenced but general/vague. | No evidence provided. |  
| \*\*Warrant / Reasoning\*\* | Logical bridge explicitly connects evidence to claim. | Reasoning restates evidence without explaining logic. | Reasoning absent or incoherent. |  
| \*\*Engagement with Counter-ideas\*\* | Accurately incorporates counterclaim or builds on peer ideas. | Acknowledges other view but treats it as a straw man. | Ignores alternative views/peer input. |  
| \*\*Revision Through Inquiry\*\* | Visibly revises or refines claim based on new inputs/talk. | Notes shift in thinking but revision is surface-level. | Claim rigid/unchanged despite conflicting evidence. |

\* \*\*Scoring:\*\* 8–10 \= Meets Standard | 6–7 \= Approaching | 0–5 \= Needs Reteach  
\* \*\*Triangulated Evidence Sources:\*\* (1) Teacher tracking data, (2) Student reflection card, (3) Group board work/VNPS notes.
